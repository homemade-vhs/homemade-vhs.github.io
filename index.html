Stream Desk

A private dashboard for the SuperMega YouTube channel's live streams.

Stream Desk runs on a small computer in our stream room and shows the hosts what is happening on the live stream, on a monitor beside them:

  • the live chat
  • a queue of Super Chats to read out on stream
  • a list of smaller supporters to thank
  • stream stats such as viewers, views and likes

It signs in to YouTube as our own channel, using Google's official YouTube Data API, to read the live chat and stream stats. The hosts can also use it to moderate the chat on their own stream. It is used only by the SuperMega team and is not offered to the public.
